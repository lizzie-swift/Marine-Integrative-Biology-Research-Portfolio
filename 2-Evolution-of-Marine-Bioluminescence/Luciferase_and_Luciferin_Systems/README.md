Luciferase diversity

The distribution of luciferase genes across marine taxa suggests a complex evolutionary history involving gene duplication, diversification and, in some lineages, potential secondary loss of bioluminescent function. The presence of luciferase homologues in non-luminous organisms indicates that bioluminescence evolution cannot always be inferred directly from gene presence alone (Delroisse et al., 2021).
