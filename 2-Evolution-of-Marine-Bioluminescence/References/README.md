Key Review Papers

Haddock, S.H.D., Moline, M.A. and Case, J.F. (2010). Bioluminescence in the Sea. Annual Review of Marine Science, 2, pp. 443-493.

Link:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21141672/

Note:
Foundational review of marine bioluminescence covering its distribution, ecological functions, evolutionary significance and importance in marine ecosystems.

Relevant to:
- Evolution of Bioluminescence
- Marine Organisms
- Future Directions



Schramm, S. and Weiß, D. (2024). Bioluminescence: The Vibrant Glow of Nature and its Chemical Mechanisms. ChemBioChem, 25(9).

Link:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38469601/

Note:
Introductory review explaining what bioluminescence is, how luciferins and luciferases produce light, the diversity of bioluminescent organisms and the underlying chemical mechanisms.

Relevant to:
- Evolution of Bioluminescence
- Luciferase and Luciferin Systems
- Marine Organisms
