Bioluminescence in the Sea. Annual Review of Marine Science, 2, pp. 443-493.

Link:
 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21141672/

Note:
 Foundational review of marine bioluminescence covering its distribution, ecological functions, evolutionary significance and importance in marine ecosystems.

Duchatelet, L. and Dupont, S. (2024)

Marine eukaryote bioluminescence: a review of species and their functional biology. Marine Life Science & Technology, 7, pp. 1-23.

Link:
 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12102453/

Note:
 Comprehensive review of marine eukaryotic bioluminescence covering taxonomic diversity, ecological functions, geographic and environmental distribution, and the evolutionary significance of light production across marine lineages. The review also compares marine and terrestrial bioluminescence and discusses hypotheses regarding the evolutionary origins of bioluminescent systems.

Bioluminescence: The Vibrant Glow of Nature and its Chemical Mechanisms. ChemBioChem, 25(9).
