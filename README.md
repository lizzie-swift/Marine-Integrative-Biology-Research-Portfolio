## 👩‍🔬 About Me
I am a Genetics graduate and NHS Genomics Technologist with interests in genomics, neuroscience, marine biology, bioinformatics and computational biology.
 
My long-term goal is to investigate how genomes and gene regulatory systems generate complex biological traits and behaviours, particularly in marine organisms.
 
## Research Theme
 
### How do genomes create complex phenotypes? 

This portfolio explores how genome evolution, gene regulation and biological networks contribute to the emergence of complex traits across marine organisms.

Areas of interest include:

- 🌍 Population genomics

- 💡 Bioluminescence
  
## Current Research Questions
### Oikopleura dioica
How do geographically distinct populations maintain similar morphology despite major differences in genome structure?

### Marine Bioluminescence
How have bioluminescent systems evolved across marine organisms and what genetic mechanisms regulate light production?

### Genomics and Neuroscience
How do gene regulatory networks contribute to the development of neural systems and complex behaviours?

### Marine Adaptation
How do marine organisms adapt genetically to environmental changes such as temperature, salinity and ocean chemistry?
